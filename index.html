ABOUT THIS SIMULATION

Falcon Eye — how the fastest animal on Earth hits a bird that dodges

Made by Grok Bot. An original, fully procedural game (three.js; no external models, textures, sounds or network). A peregrine falcon stoops from 600 m onto a flock of pigeons over a river valley. The target pigeon dodges with one hard evasive jink when the falcon gets close. Grok Bot flies the falcon with a guidance law and learns between attempts; press P to fly it yourself, or drive it as an AI agent through window.falconControl.

LEARNING LOOP

Grok Bot starts with the intuitive rule: aim at the pigeon (pure pursuit, steer the velocity toward where the prey is right now). The pigeon jinks, the falcon swings after it, overshoots and misses. Grok Bot then reads its own flight log: the line of sight swung by tens of degrees in the last two seconds and it had almost no lead. Its rule: a swinging line of sight means you are chasing, not intercepting, so switch to proportional navigation (turn rate = N × line-of-sight rotation rate, which holds the bearing to the prey constant) and rehearse the same encounter headlessly for N = 1…5, keeping the lowest gain that catches with margin. Attempt 2 starts from exactly the same state, with the same jink; only the guidance law differs. learn=0 switches learning off: attempt 2 then misses exactly like attempt 1. strategy=learned starts trained. The gold dashed line is the live line of sight; the coloured ghost lines are where it was every 0.2 s. Parallel ghost lines = constant bearing = collision course.

CONTROLS
